Ethereum clear signing over `@hzblj/crypto-ethereum` 0.3.0: `clearSignTransaction`, `clearSignTypedData` and `clearSignMessage` read transactions of every type, EIP-712 typed data and personal messages through the chain client into `@hzblj/crypto-clear-sign`'s display model, with ERC-7730 descriptors from a resolver.
